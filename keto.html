Over the years I’ve experimented with a Keto diet. Here are my rough notes on the topic.

Target Carbs

Under 30g is best to trigger keto. 50g is the absolute maximum, and can risk being in a middle ground where you can’t trigger ketosis, but end up relying on gluconeogenesis instead, which can come from amino acides from your muscles.

Ketones will use beta-oxidation from fats directly, avoiding gluconeogenisis.

So ultimately you should target something low enough to trigger ketosis, and regularly measure your ketosis (I’m going to use a breathalyzer) to ensure your are staying in that state, if you are not in complete control of your diet.

Keto Gotchas

Be careful of high saturated fat

Keto diets, because they are high in fat, tend to be very high in saturated fat. There is a direct correlation with saturated fat intake and LDL cholesterol and APOb, which are in turn related to artherosclerosis and heart disease.

Some saturated fat is unavoidable, but I try to keep it as low as possible, and intentionally choose foods that are low in saturated fat.

Gum

Most gums are sugar free. You have to be careful about a lot of gum, which may have maltitol syrup - this can cause an insulin response.

Good kinds:

  • Trident
    • Cinnamon
    • Pineapple Twist
  • Extra

Bad kinds:

  • Trident Vibes (basically any flavor)

Huel Black

17-19g carbs per 400kcal meal. So eat it in 1/2 servings.

  • Unflavored is the best to use, as it’s 7.5g carbs per 200kcal. I mix it with a stevia-based sweetener.

Recipes

Slimfast high protein + hemp hearts

  • 1 slimfast high protein (26-28g)
  • 16g hemp hearts
  • 360g water

17g protein, 200kcal, 3g net carbs, 0.75g saturated fat

Keto tortilla + morning star crumbles

  • 55g morning star vegan crumbles (0 net carbs)
  • 1 small mission zero net sun-dried tomatoes (25kcal, 0 net carbs)
  • 15g G Hughes ketchup or other dressing (1 net carb)
    • if you want more calories other G Hughes sauce is also 1-2 net carbs with much higher calories content.

15g protein, 120kcal, 2g net carbs, 0 saturated fat

17g protein, 95kcal, 2g net carbs, 0 saturated fat
