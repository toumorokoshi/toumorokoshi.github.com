Keto

Over the years I’ve experimented with a Keto diet. Here are my rough notes on the topic.

Target Carbs

Under 30g is best to trigger keto. 50g is the absolute maximum, and can risk being in a middle ground where you can’t trigger ketosis, but end up relying on gluconeogenesis instead, which can come from amino acides from your muscles.

Ketones will use beta-oxidation from fats directly, avoiding gluconeogenisis.

So ultimately you should target something low enough to trigger ketosis, and regularly measure your ketosis (I’m going to use a breathalyzer) to ensure your are staying in that state, if you are not in complete control of your diet.

Gum

Most gums are sugar free. You have to be careful about a lot of gum, which may have maltitol syrup - this can cause an insulin response.

Good kinds:

  • Trident
    • Cinnamon
    • Pineapple Twist

Bad kinds:

  • Triden Vibes (basically any flavor)

Huel Black

17-19g carbs per 400kcal meal. So eat it in 1/2 servings.

  • Unflavored is 7.5g carbs per 200kcal.
